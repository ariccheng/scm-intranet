Your account is set up. Here is a quick guide to talking with your colleagues.

## Who you can reach
Open **Contacts** to see everyone. Each card shows a status:

- **Available**: you can message this colleague now.
- **Email only**: Daniel Alvarez, our CEO, writes to the team by email and is not available for chat. For questions, message a colleague who is Available.
- **Out of office**: not available yet. More colleagues become available over the semester; an email from Jordan and a notification on your phone tell you when.

This mailbox (IT Service Desk) only sends notices and is not listed in Contacts.

## How messaging works
- **Message** in Contacts, or **Reply** in Mail, opens a chat with that colleague in a new tab, in the university's AI tool.
- Your colleagues are AI assistants playing a role. Each one knows their own job, not everything.
- **Jordan Lee** (HR & Front Desk) has no question limit. Other colleagues answer a set number of questions each week; the weekly allowance is shown on their contact card. Plan your questions before you start.

## Good habits
- Do not share personal information, passwords or grades in a chat.
- Write down who you asked, what you asked and what you learned in your team's **communication log** (Files, Templates folder).
- If a chat does not open, tell your instructor.
